TF-QKD / FSO Research Framework

Open-source Python, documented with MkDocs.

PythonCVXPYTF-QKD
  • Modular implementation of the Curty–Azuma–Lo Protocol-3 TF-QKD security proof: X and Z basis gain and QBER, cat-state phase-error estimation, an asymptotic secret-key-rate pipeline and a PLOB repeaterless-bound comparison.
  • Extended to a finite-decoy linear-programming framework (CVXPY) for finite-decoy yield and phase-error bounds, with convergence, feasibility and finite-versus-infinite-decoy consistency diagnostics.
  • Built deterministic and log-normal-turbulence FSO channel models: Beer–Lambert attenuation, Gaussian beam propagation, Rytov variance and Monte Carlo channel realisations for ergodic key-rate estimation.
  • Developing an analytical log-normal averaging framework to replace Monte-Carlo-only averaging, keeping Monte Carlo for validation.

QSAFire

Satellite-to-ground SNS-TF-QKD simulation package, built at the Scientific Analysis Group, Defence Research and Development Organisation.

PythonSNS-TF-QKDOrbitsMonte CarloStreamlit
  • Independent Python package for sending-or-not-sending twin-field QKD (SNS-TF-QKD) over three channels: fiber, free-space optics with Gamma-Gamma turbulence, and a ground-to-satellite uplink. It implements the asymptotic key-rate formula of Wang, Yu and Hu (Phys. Rev. A 98, 062323).
  • The protocol equations live in one module and are reused unchanged by every channel model, so no physics is duplicated.
  • The satellite link is built in separable layers: WGS-84 geodesy and a solved sun-synchronous orbit, Rayleigh, aerosol and gas optical depth, a Hufnagel-Valley turbulence profile with the spherical-wave Rytov variance, and seasonal datasets for winter, summer and monsoon.
  • Hardware and environment are modeled explicitly: Friis diffraction loss, Gaussian pointing jitter, a characterised avalanche photodiode, day and night background light, and weather-gated annual availability from rainy-day and fog climatology.
  • Joint optimisation of signal intensity, decoy intensity and sending probability, with a Monte Carlo ensemble that folds turbulence fading into the real-loss channel.
  • A six-city link database and a fully asymmetric, per-arm version of the rate formulas, applied across four city-pair links. A Streamlit dashboard shows the whole chain.
  • Each stage is checked against an outside reference. Orbital solver and elevation–slant-range formulas agree with Landsat-8/9, Sentinel-2 and published Micius data to within 1–3%, and the Gamma-Gamma average is cross-validated between quadrature and Monte Carlo.

Climate-Aware Quantum Channel Modeling

Satellite QKD channel model from reanalysis data.

ERA5CAMSBB84
  • Physics-consistent satellite-to-ground optical channel using ERA5 meteorological and CAMS aerosol reanalysis data. It models Rayleigh scattering, aerosol extinction, atmospheric absorption, turbulence and geometric beam-spreading loss.
  • Integrated these effects into decoy-state BB84 simulations to evaluate QBER and secure key rate.
  • Benchmarked wavelength-dependent performance across geographically diverse Indian ground stations.

Decoy-State BB84 Satellite QKD Constellation Simulator

Satellite QKD constellation simulator behind a first-author paper in the International Journal of Information Technology (Springer, 2025).

PythonSatellite QKDBB84Streamlit
  • Simulates a decoy-state BB84 QKD network over a Walker-Delta LEO constellation, for a 24-hour period and five Indian ground stations: Hanle, Dehradun, Mt Abu, Shillong and Kodaikanal.
  • Pure-Python Keplerian and J2 orbit propagator with a Walker-Delta generator and TLE export, and an aperture-based optical channel with atmospheric transmittance that depends on elevation.
  • Computes secret key rate and QBER from first principles, with coverage windows, link-budget breakdowns and nine kinds of publication-quality figure.
  • Command-line interface, YAML configuration, Streamlit dashboard and unit and integration tests. Default run: 3 planes of 4 satellites at 600 km, with 1.3–1.7% of the day usable per station.

SateSim

Satellite communication link-budget simulator with a dashboard. Self-implemented from lectures by Prof. A. Al-Hourani (RMIT University).

PythonSkyfieldStreamlitPlotlyBPSK / QPSK
  • Orbit propagation with SGP4 through Skyfield, vectorised across several satellites, feeds an RF link budget built from free-space path loss and the Friis transmission equation.
  • Computes SNR, bit error rate for BPSK and QPSK, Doppler shift from range-rate projection, and elevation, with visibility filtering by minimum elevation.
  • Physics lives in one UI-free module. A Streamlit and Plotly dashboard and a command-line runner share it, so results can be reproduced either way.
  • Exports CSV datasets, PNG plots and a multi-page technical PDF report with equations, figures and analysis. MIT licensed.

Paddy Microclimate Stress Classifier

Course project for CMU 11-785 Deep Learning.

PyTorchBiLSTM
  • BiLSTM–LSTM model for three-class paddy stress forecasting across six Indian agroclimatic zones, from 48-hour weather sequences of temperature, relative humidity, VPD, ET0 and soil moisture.
  • Leave-one-location-out testing on unseen coastal tropical climates.
  • Open-sourced with post-hoc attention analysis.

VeriSure AI

Insurance policy question answering, built for the HackRx 6.0 problem statement (Bajaj Finserv Hackathon).

PythonFastAPIFAISSGeminiStreamlit
  • Upload one or more insurance policy documents (PDF or DOCX) and ask questions in plain language, such as whether a procedure is covered.
  • Each session gets its own FAISS index. Documents are chunked and embedded with a MiniLM sentence-transformer, and a question retrieves the most relevant clauses.
  • Gemini 1.5 Flash reads those clauses and returns a structured JSON decision with an amount, a justification and the clauses it relied on.
  • FastAPI backend with upload and query endpoints, a Streamlit front end, and a Procfile for deployment on Railway.

VibeSense

Fashion and aesthetic detection from short videos, built for the Flickd AI Hackathon 2025.

PythonYOLOv8CLIPFAISSSentence Transformers
  • Backend engine that takes a short video, such as a reel, and returns the clothing products in it and the style it expresses, for example Coquette, Streetwear or Vintage.
  • Pipeline: FFmpeg frame extraction, YOLOv8n detection trained on the Fashionpedia dataset (45 clothing categories), cropping of detected items, and CLIP embeddings of each crop.
  • Products are matched against a pre-indexed catalog with cosine-similarity FAISS search. Vibes come from the video caption using MiniLM embeddings.
  • Output is structured JSON with the vibe tags and each matched product, its type, colour, match type and confidence.

DeepSpeak

AI persona chatbot built from a person’s messages, photos and voice notes, for CodeClash 2.0. Team of four.

PythonFastAPIWhisperStable DiffusionStreamlit
  • Users upload text chats, photos and voice recordings related to a loved one who has died, and the system builds a chatbot that answers in that person’s style.
  • My part was the AI component: Whisper transcription of audio, Tesseract OCR, personality and tone analysis from the text, face detection for the visual profile, and the chatbot on top of OpenAI, DeepSeek or Gemini models.
  • Stable Diffusion generates portraits, SQLite stores profiles and chat history, and profiles can be exported as JSON. Streamlit and FastAPI interfaces, deployed on Vercel.
  • Built with Naivedya Giri, Krish Tanwar and Vasu Chaudhary, who led the front end and full-stack work.
