Privacy policy

What this site collects: nothing.

No cookies, no analytics, no forms and no accounts. This page explains the few outside services your browser contacts when you visit, and how to reach me.

Last updated 17 October 2026

This is the personal website of Anand Kumar, a student researcher in New Delhi, India. It is a static site: it shows pages and files and does not ask you for anything.

What I collect

I do not collect personal data through this site. It has:

  • no cookies and no browser storage (the pages do not use localStorage or similar),
  • no analytics, advertising, tracking pixels or fingerprinting,
  • no forms, comments, accounts or newsletter sign-up.

I do not receive visitor statistics of any kind.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may process technical data such as your IP address, browser details and the pages you request, in order to deliver the site and keep it secure. I do not have access to those logs. GitHub explains what it does in its privacy statement.

Outside services your browser contacts

Some pages ask other companies' servers for a file. Your browser then shares your IP address and browser details with that server, as it would for any website.

  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com), on every page, to load the typefaces. See Google's privacy policy.
  • cdnjs, run by Cloudflare (cdnjs.cloudflare.com), on the CV page, to load the PDF viewer, and on a few blog posts, to draw equations. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Nothing else is loaded from outside this site. The CV viewer opens the PDF from this site's own files. If you prefer, you can skip the viewer and download the PDF directly.

If you email me

Email links on this site open your own mail app. If you write to me, I receive your email address and whatever you choose to send. I use it only to reply and keep it only as long as the conversation needs. I do not sell it, share it for marketing or add you to any list. My mail is handled by Gmail (Google).

Links to other sites

This site links to other places where I publish or have profiles, such as GitHub, LinkedIn, Google Scholar, ResearchGate, publishers and DOI pages. Those sites have their own privacy practices, which apply once you leave this one.

My CV

The CV I offer for download lists my email address, my institutions and links to my public profiles. It does not include a phone number or home address.

Children

This site is about research and is not aimed at children. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Your rights

Because the site does not collect personal data, there is nothing for me to look up or delete. Depending on where you live, such as India, the EU or the UK, you may have rights over personal data held about you. For anything I hold from emails you sent me, you can ask me to show it, correct it or delete it, and I will.

Changes

If this changes, for example if I add analytics, I will update this page and the date at the top before the change goes live.
